Deepawali, also known as Diwali, is the festival of lights. It is one of the most important festivals in India. People clean and decorate their homes with lamps, candles, and rangoli. This festival marks the return of Lord Rama to Ayodhya after defeating Ravana. People worship Goddess Lakshmi and Lord Ganesha for wealth and happiness. Everyone wears new clothes, exchanges sweets, and enjoys fireworks. Diwali teaches us that good always wins over evil and light over darkness. We should celebrate it happily and in an eco-friendly way
